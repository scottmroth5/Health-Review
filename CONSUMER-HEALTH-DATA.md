# Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

This policy covers health information in Health Review, a personal project run by its owner (the operator). The public demo holds made-up data only.

## What is collected and why

- Apple Health data you send, such as activity, heart rate, sleep, workouts and body measurements.
- Check-ins, weight and body composition, drinks and CBD use, medications and supplements with their doses, lab results and workouts you enter or import.
- Which doses you mark taken or skipped, and the targets, heart rate zones and goals you set.
- Your birth year, sex and height, if you add them.
- Anything health related you write in your notes, prompt sections, chat messages or feedback, which may include medical history or genetic information.
- Weekly reviews, trends and suggestions the app and AI create from this data, which may reveal health information.

This data is used to show your information back to you, compute your metrics and targets, write your reviews and answers if AI is on, send account notices, keep backups, and keep the app secure and working. It is used for nothing else.

## Where it comes from

You; your iPhone through Health Auto Export; a Google Sheet or file you connect or upload; and the app and AI, which derive it from what you provide.

## Who receives it

Your consumer health data is never sold. It is not shared with anyone other than these services, which handle it to run the app:

- **Amazon Web Services:** stores all categories above on the server.
- **Anthropic:** when AI is on for you, receives computed summaries (including your medications with their doses and changes, and your latest lab results with their ranges), your notes for the week, your prompt sections, your Next program chat messages and Help questions, and, if supplement suggestions are on, your supplements, medication names and latest labs with each chat message.
- **Google:** holds encrypted backups the operator's key alone can open, and delivers any feedback you send to the operator by email.

There are no affiliates. No third party collects your health data across websites or online services through this app. PostHog analytics runs only in the demo and never in the app.

## Your rights

You can ask whether your consumer health data is collected, shared or sold; get a copy of it; get a list of who has received it, with a contact email for each; withdraw your consent; and have it deleted.

- **Export:** Settings, Export my data.
- **Delete or withdraw consent:** Settings, Delete my account removes your data at once. Copies in encrypted backups age out within about two months. Feedback emails already sent to the operator stay in the operator's mailbox until the operator deletes them. Withdrawing consent means the app can no longer hold your health data, so it is handled as deleting your account.
- **Anything else:** email {{SUPPORT}}. You will get an answer within 45 days. If more time is needed, you will be told why, and it will be at most 45 more days.

## Appeals

If a request is refused, reply to that email with the word Appeal within 30 days. You will get a written decision with reasons within 45 days. If the appeal is denied, you can contact your state attorney general.

## Changes

Material changes will be emailed to approved users and posted here with a new effective date. New uses of your data will not begin until you consent to them.
