# Entering data in the app

Some things are entered right in Health Review. Everything here is yours only.

## Daily Check-in

- **Check-in:** readiness, energy, mood, stress and nutrition on a 1 to 10 scale, plus optional weight and body measurements and a note. A day or two of these gives the weekly review a feel for how you are doing.
- **Medications and supplements:** tick what you took each day. Use **Adjust** for a one-day change of dose or time.
- **Drinks:** beer, wine, spirits and other drinks, and CBD (never counted as alcohol). Logging a day with zero drinks counts as an alcohol-free day; a day you don't log is unknown.

## Meds

- Add each medication or supplement with its dose and when you take it. A dose change is a new dated period, so the history shows how things changed. Medication topics in the review only ever go to its "Discuss with your physician" list.

## Labs

- Add lab results by test and draw date. Each test shows a typical range you can change to your lab's own. Lab results are discussed only in the physician list, never turned into advice.

## Health tab profile

- Your birth year, sex and height set the suggested targets, and your Zone 2 heart rate range sets the heart rate zones.
