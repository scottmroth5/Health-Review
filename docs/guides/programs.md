# Your training programs

Add the programs you follow, and Health Review can show which phase and week you are in, rank what to run next, and compare what a workout called for with what you logged. Open **Programs** on Home.

## Three ways to add one

- **New program:** type it in. Give it a name, its length in weeks, and its phases (for example Phase 1 for weeks 1 to 4, Phase 2 for weeks 5 to 8), each with its workouts and their exercises, sets and reps. Reps can be a number (`5`), a range (`8-12`) or seconds for a hold (`30s`).
- **Import JSON:** a file made by **Export mine** (or by hand in the same format). Nothing is added unless every program in it checks out.
- **Read a PDF with Claude:** choose the program's PDF and tick that you own it or have the right to use it. Claude reads the program's structure with your AI (your Claude key, or the administrator's allowance if you have one), and it opens in the builder for you to check against the PDF before you save. The PDF is not kept, and a long program can cost about a dollar.

## Tying your log to a program

- **Match words:** a Workout Log "Workout" name containing any of a program's match words belongs to it. For example, with match words `5x5, garage`, a day named `Garage 5x5 Phase 1 A` counts. With no match words, the program's name is used.
- **Phases:** write the phase in the Workout name (`Phase 1`, `Phase 2`) and Health Review follows it, even when you miss days and the calendar drifts.

## Program blocks

Each run of a program found in your log is a **block**. Confirm a block on the Programs screen so the Training tab's Current program card, phases and progress follow it.

## Shared programs

If the administrator shares their program catalog with you, its program names are listed on the Programs screen. Their phases and lengths are used for you, but their contents are not shown or exported. A program of your own with the same name is used instead.
