Use Health Review with your own data

This demo shows made-up data. The real Health Review turns your Apple Health data, workouts and daily check-ins into charts, tracks your goals and training programs, and can write you a weekly review with AI if you add your own Claude key.

It's free and by invitation: sign in with Google, say what you'll use, and you'll get an email when you're approved, usually within a day. Your data is private to you.
