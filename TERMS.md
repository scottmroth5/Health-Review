# Terms of Use

Health Review is a personal project run by its owner, shared with a few people by invitation. By requesting access you agree to these terms.

## Not medical advice

- Health Review shows your own data back to you and, if you add a Claude API key, writes a weekly review of it with Anthropic's Claude.
- Nothing in the app or its reviews is medical advice. Talk to your doctor about medications, lab results, symptoms and any change to your health.

## Your account

- Access is approved by hand and can be paused or ended at any time.
- You can delete your account and all of your data at any time.
- Keep your Google account secure; it is how you sign in.

## Your Claude API key

- AI features use only your own Claude API key, and you pay Anthropic for that use. Without a key, no AI is used.
- Your key is stored encrypted and is never shown again after you enter it. Remove it any time in Settings.
