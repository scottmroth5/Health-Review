# Terms of Use

Health Review is a personal project run by its owner, shared with a few people by invitation. By requesting access you agree to these terms.

## Not medical advice

- Health Review shows your own data back to you and, if you use AI, writes a weekly review of it with Anthropic's Claude.
- Nothing in the app, its reviews, the chat or Help's answers is medical advice. Talk to your doctor about medications, lab results, symptoms and any change to your health.

## Your account

- Access is approved by hand and can be paused or ended at any time.
- You can export your data, pause your account, or delete your account and all of your data at any time in Settings. Encrypted backups keep a copy until they age out, within about two months.
- Add only programs and files you own or have the right to use.
- Keep your Google account secure; it is how you sign in.

## AI and your Claude API key

- AI features use your own Claude API key, and you pay Anthropic for that use. The operator may instead cover your AI up to a monthly limit, which can end at any time. Without either, no AI is used.
- Your key is stored encrypted and is never shown again after you enter it. Remove it any time in Settings.

## No warranty

- The app is provided as is, without any warranty. It may change, be unavailable, or lose data. Keep your own copies of anything important.
